LeadOps

AI lead qualification for real-estate websites. It scores every visitor, blocks bots, fake numbers and rival brokers, and shows the sales team which buyers are ready, with the reasons in plain words.

  • Machine learning: a LightGBM classifier on 27 behavioural signals (mouse movement, scroll depth, timing) tells people from bots, stealth bots included.
  • LLM assistant: replies in English, Hinglish, Hindi and Marathi, pulls budget and location from free text, and sorts visitors into buyer, investor, tenant, broker or spam.
  • Responsible AI: phone numbers and emails are masked before any text reaches the LLM; built for India's DPDP Act 2023.
  • Human-in-the-loop: sales marks leads Genuine, Fake or Converted, and the next training run learns from it.

YouTube ChatApp

Ask any YouTube video a question and get an answer grounded in its transcript.

  1. Fetch the transcript with youtube-transcript-api and a custom regex URL parser
  2. Split it with RecursiveCharacterTextSplitter
  3. Embed the chunks with HuggingFace embeddings
  4. Search them semantically in a FAISS vector store
  5. Answer with an LLM in a Streamlit chat UI
